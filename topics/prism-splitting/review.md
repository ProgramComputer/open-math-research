# Prior work and review

The precise target is the second clause of Ferroni and McGinnis, *Lattice points in slices of prisms*, Conjecture 5.12. Their printed condition is a positive bound vector and a last bound at least two before splitting. The text expressly allows the resulting all-unit hypersimplex. The classical full-support transportation real-rootedness conjecture and the first clause of Conjecture 5.12 make claims about individual polynomials. The counterexample bears on the stronger pairwise assertion. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X24000233) · [Public preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.11808) · [Transportation discussion](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627)

On 25 November 2026, primary-source, citation-index and GitHub searches located the original conjecture and related investigations. No earlier refutation of this split was located. A signed-out browser could inspect issues and files, while global GitHub code search required sign-in. Direct Google Scholar queries encountered verification pages. This is a scoped public-priority search.

The search engine used a dynamic program on bounded compositions. An independent calculation used subset inclusion–exclusion on all coordinates, and an additional check used grouped binomial terms. All three recovered the two degree-nine and degree-ten coefficient vectors. Exact sign changes in rational intervals certify every root. Independent reviewers checked the geometric dimensions, the quantifier permitting the split to all units, and the interior counts yielding the infinite-family obstruction. At $a=7$, the interior counts in dilation three are 3498 and 3432, and the resulting next leading coefficients are 3485 and 3418.

This is internal project review, including independent GPT-6.2 Sol checks, as distinct from external peer review. Source and finite certificates were rerun in a clean process.
