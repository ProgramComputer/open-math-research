## Prefix and tail arithmetic

The recurrence for $E$ computes through exponent 48 modulo $t^{49}$. Differentiation followed by multiplication by $t$ cannot make an omitted exponent contribute to this prefix. Dividing by $t$ then leaves degrees zero through 47, with the first omitted term coming from $E$ exponent 49. In $I=t(1-t)E'+ntE$, the monomial $t^p$ contributes $p t^p+(n-p)t^{p+1}$, both with nonnegative coefficients. The lag $J$ also has nonnegative coefficients. Consequently the absolute coefficients of $I-J$ are bounded by those of $I+J$, including in the four-cycle next rows. The word bound gives the first omitted majorant term $49^{n+1}x^{48}$ and all successive ratios are at most $x(50/49)^{n+1}$. The code certifies the denominator strictly positive before division and accepts signs only on strict Arb separation from the tail enclosure.
