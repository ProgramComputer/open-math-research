# Independent scope and assembly review

Checked November 26, 2026. Alexandersson’s 2026 SymCat conjecture concerns every fixed integer $k\ge3$, every lower size $n\ge k$, and the original unweighted polynomial $`E_{n,k}(t)=\sum_{\pi}t^{\#\{i:\pi(i)>i\}}`$ over permutations of $[n]$ all of whose cycles have length at least $k$. It asks that the rows have only real zeros and interlace consecutively. The catalog defines interlacing weakly. The cases $k=1$ (ordinary Eulerian) and $k=2$ (derangement, with the known insertion proof noted by SymCat) provide context outside the conjecture. For $1\le n<k$ the sum is zero and $E_{0,k}=1$ by the empty permutation. The claim starts at $n=k$, so the zero/constant prefix and the transition from the zero polynomial to the first nonzero row require no theorem. [Primary statement](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjLongCycleExcedances) · [Interlacing definition](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedInterlacing.htm)

For every admissible size, each cycle contributes at least one excedance and the unique descending cycle with one ascent contributes the coefficient of $t$. Its inverse supplies the unique permutation with $n-1$ excedances. Thus $E_{n,k}=tQ_{n,k}$ with $Q_{n,k}(0)=1$ and degree $n-2$. When a proof supplies strict simple negative interlacing of $Q_{n,k},Q_{n+1,k}$ with the larger row supplying both outer roots, appending the simple zero to both lists gives exactly weak interlacing of the original rows. This also proves individual real-rootedness from the first admissible row onward.
