## Join and signs

Let $H=\lfloor7\sqrt n/20\rfloor$, $\theta=(H+5/6)\pi/n$ and $b=\pi/\tan\theta$. The join geometry and phase bound give $b\ge0$, $b^2+\pi^2\le n\pi^2$, and $\phi_n(b)<(H+1)\pi$. The modulus-separation lemma gives $(459/40)(H/(H+1))^n<e^{-b}$, with $459/40=(9/8)(51/5)$. It suffices for all exterior moduli to lie below this explicit front bound. Equivalently, with strict ordering it suffices to bound the last exterior modulus by the same bound, allowing equality there. The old integer-angle estimate at $(H+1)\pi/n$ in the front note is separate from this smaller-angle seam and cannot substitute for it.

The center phase is exactly $n\pi/2$. The middle selector uses lower phase levels $j=H+1,\ldots,\lfloor n/2\rfloor-1$ and upper levels $j+1$. The modulus decreases with logarithmic coordinate, while the phase also decreases with that coordinate. Thus increasing $j$ orders the selected moduli increasingly in $(e^{-b},1)$. The join inequality and the front bound make the concatenation strictly increasing. The number of below-unit roots is $H+(\lfloor n/2\rfloor-H-1)=\lfloor(n-2)/2\rfloor$. For $n\ge10000$ the proposed $H$ is smaller than $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$.

At exterior zero-based index $i$ the literal successor has sign $(-1)^{i+2}$. In middle local index $a$, the lower phase level is $j=H+1+a$. The literal successor sign is $(-1)^{j+1}=(-1)^{H+a+2}$, as required at global index $i=H+a$. Since $E_{n+1,k}(-u)=(-u)Q(-u)$ for the reduced successor $Q$, dividing by the negative parameter reverses every sign and yields $(-1)^{i+1}$. In particular the first middle raw sign $(-1)^{H+2}$ becomes $(-1)^{H+1}$ after division. The last exterior reduced sign is $(-1)^H$. Alternation therefore crosses the seam.
