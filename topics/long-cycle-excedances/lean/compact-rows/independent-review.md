# Independent review of the compact row enclosure

An agent working independently of the proof author checked the original definitions, scope, packed representation, and execution records. The source commit reviewed was `8c71bc6f0a3ca68bd930fff6163fb534165b5192`.

The formal coefficient is the coefficient of degree $j$ of the literal strict-excedance enumerator divided by $X$. The lower coefficient starts with the Eulerian row on $k-1$ labels, rounded down. Each insertion step uses the exact Eulerian weights. The zero constant of the original nonempty row is removed once. A negative index multiplier after natural subtraction is harmless because its original coefficient counts more than $n$ excedances and hence vanishes.

The theorem `compact_all_original` applies at every index and size, for $k\ge2$ and $b\ge1$. The lower estimate can become vacuous when $n2^{1-b}\ge1$. The theorem `compact_original_prefix_error` applies to any prefix width and every real $y\ge0$ when $b=48$ and $n\le1001$. The signed prefix error is at most $2^{-36}$ times the positive rounded prefix. These statements concern the recurrence defined in Lean.
