# Linear envelopes for the literal occupied primitive

The marked-bin bound starts from the actual primitive factor in the [paired-profile expansion](../paired-profile/occupied-values.md). Its degree $s$ coefficient has absolute value $|b_s|\cdot|z_q|^{s-1}/(|a_q-a|S)$ for signed odd indices $a_q$ and $a$. The [bivariate bridge](marked-bins.md) marks primitive degree and summed bin separately. This package gives the input `hL` to its rounded product estimate.

`original_checker_linear_envelope` uses the [literal numerator verification](../paired-profile/numerator-verification.md) to supply the coefficient identity and all size-block bounds, including the atom in bin four and the jump in bin eight. The width-four bin holds only degree $k$ at bin four. For bins five through eight the integer floor endpoints telescope. In later bins the factorial index is $\lfloor(i-1)/4\rfloor$ and the bin has at most $k/4+1$ sizes. No primitive beyond the chosen cap enters those coefficients.

At cutoff $k\ge K\ge193$, the power $s-1$ in each primitive is at least 192. The pointwise power-96 Bernoulli bound gives the shape used by the two rational checkers. The exact off-diagonal signed gap is used before summing over poles. Each distinguished pole outside the finite head can be moved to the nearest external center for the head estimate. The omitted signed mass has the exact tail denominator $190cP$. The proof uses a discrete Bernoulli inequality followed by telescoping in each odd half-lattice. Its guard holds uniformly for $K\ge208$, $X\le48$, and a signed cutoff $P\ge63$. The star tail pays an additional factor $1/2$ from the odd gaps.

The Lean interface takes the particular finite head, scalar tail and outward rounding inequalities as hypotheses. The exact Python replay independently reconstructs and compares every linear bin through 240 in all nine slabs at the original denominator $2^{56}$ and at the independent denominator $2^{52}$. These particular finite table values still require a Lean input certificate. The principal carrier, quantitative occupied value error, final adjacent sign comparison, and full conjecture remain outside the scope of this package.
