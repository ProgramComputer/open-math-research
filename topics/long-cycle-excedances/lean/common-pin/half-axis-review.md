# Independent scope review of half-axis assembly

An independent agent-assisted review checked the proof against the
original consecutive-size predicate. It uses the literal polynomials at
sizes $n$ and $n+1$. The reflected intervals exchange the two reciprocal
endpoints and reverse their index order. They remain strictly separated,
including across the center. Reciprocal reflection preserves the old
opposite-sign condition. It changes the next signed value by the sign
of the next degree parity.

For odd old degree the proof constructs the central old root at minus
one and requires the corresponding next-row sign. For even old degree
there is no central input, including degree zero. The complete left-side
windows and their signs are hypotheses of the checked implication.
