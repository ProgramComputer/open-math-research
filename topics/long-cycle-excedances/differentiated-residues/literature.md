# Prior-art check

**November 26, 2026.** Per Alexandersson's catalog entry “Permutations with long cycles” asks whether the excedance polynomials for permutations with every cycle of length at least $k$ are real-rooted and interlace consecutively for each fixed $k\ge3$. The catalog separately discusses the descent statistic and its different interlacing question. The present theorem gives a sufficient range for the nonzero excedance roots. [Original statement](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjLongCycleExcedances) · [Tracking issue](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/issues/4)

The closest project results are the signed occupied-pole bounds, the wider contour for actual consecutive rows, and the adaptive table with uniform cutoff $4{,}062{,}720$. The differentiated-residue estimate uses the signed product identities but separates the derivative of the common pole multiplier from the derivative of its coefficient error. This supplies the insertion comparison in angles between the earlier exterior and adaptive middle ranges.
