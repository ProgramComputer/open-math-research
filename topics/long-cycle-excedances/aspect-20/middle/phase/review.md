# Independent check of the vertical pole estimate

The independent agent-assisted review checked $251\le n\le9999$, $k\ge4$, $n/k\ge20$, and $n\sin^2\theta\ge1$. The height support, common pole power $n+1$, ratio of the two numerator values, and the sign and orientation of the phase join agree with the generating-function normalization. The factor for the vertical segment uses length $(p-1)\pi$. The density term $e^{-as}(as)^{k-1}/(k-1)!$ attains its maximum at its mode or at the nearer interval endpoint.

The [independent program](check-independent.py) uses Arb with 110-bit precision, angular boxes of width two and a geometric ratio for the omitted density frequencies. Once $a>2(k-1)/l$ on height interval $[l,h]$, the successive term ratio is bounded by $\exp(-l/2)$. Retaining the first term of the tail and summing this geometric majorant avoids invoking a Poisson tail inequality at this step. All 64,962 nonempty angular boxes gave weighted nonprincipal error below $0.09$. The default primary replay proves $0.1$; either bound suffices for the stated contrast. The additional stronger primary bound $0.08$ uses a separate finer replay. The exterior endpoint uses $H+5/6$ and the printed trigonometric and gamma inequalities enter with the needed signs. Formal coverage is ⬜ None.
