# Sources and prior work on short peaks

Per Alexandersson states that the polynomials counting strict local maxima in Catalan words are real-rooted and interlace consecutively. The same statement gives the binomial expansion about $t=1$ and the quadratic generating function. The coefficient triangle is A116424 and also counts occurrences of $UDUU$ in Dyck paths. [Conjecture](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjCatalanWordPeaks) · [OEIS A116424](https://oeis.org/A116424)

Jean-Luc Baril, Pamela E. Harris, Kimberly J. Harry, Matt McClinton and José L. Ramírez derive the generating function in Theorem 5.1 of *Enumerating runs, valleys, and peaks in Catalan words*, published online March 19, 2025. The length-one peak marks the strict peak considered here. The preprint appeared April 8, 2024. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.26493/2590-9770.1794.fc5) · [Preprint and versions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05672)

The research program at 1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures records an argument for eventual real-rootedness and consecutive interlacing on November 24, 2026. Its four-branch-point description, eventual signs and endpoint analyses supply the starting point of our proof. It leaves an unspecified finite prefix. The contribution here is an effective bound of $150$, exact adjacent-row sign estimates throughout the middle ranks, effective extreme-root comparisons, and an exact prefix meeting that bound.
