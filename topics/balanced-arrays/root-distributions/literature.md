# Sources and prior results

Per Alexandersson states the conjecture for labeled binary arrays with constant column sums and a uniform row cap. On November 26, 2026, the author's GitHub tracking issue was open. The claim in the catalogue concerns real-rootedness. The present work proves finite and limiting counts of root moduli for that polynomial. The limits can be proved without the project's eventual real-rootedness theorem. [Symmetric Functions Catalog](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays) · [Author's tracking issue](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2005) · [Earlier eventual theorem](../eventual-all/README.md)

Ulrich Abel, Wolfgang Gawronski and Thorsten Neuschel studied the unrestricted binomial polynomial with fixed exponent as its degree tends to infinity. Their paper gives a limiting zero distribution in that setting. Here both degree parameter and exponent can grow, and a proper row cap couples the columns. The fixed-cap and fixed-corank limits involve different truncated Poisson means. *Computational Methods and Function Theory* **13** (2013), 163–180, published online March 20. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40315-013-0013-3) · [Author-hosted manuscript](https://doras.dcu.ie/31197/1/Binomialpolynomials.pdf)

The fact that balancing margins increases the number of binary matrices is a majorization result for bipartite degree sequences. Annabell Berger's 2012 preprint treats this result. The manuscript gives a two-column counting proof for the case used here. The second input is Jensen's formula for the circular mean of the logarithm of a polynomial. Our contribution is the combination of these bounds at the relevant scales, the resulting finite root-count estimate, and the five limits for the capped arrays. [Berger's preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.5443)
