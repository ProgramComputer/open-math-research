# Prior-art check

**November 26, 2026.** Per Alexandersson's conjecture asks whether, for every $n\ge0$ and $1\le r\le d$, the polynomial counting labeled zero-one arrays by their common column quota, with each row sum at most $r$, has only real zeros. The catalog entry and the author's issue give the same parameters and the same diagonal coefficient formula. The proposed partial theorem gives simple negative zeros under a specific effective sufficient condition. [SymCat, “Balanced arrays of uniform matroids”](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays) · [Author's open-problem issue](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2005) · [Project tracking issue](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/issues/2)

The exact comparison is an improvement from effective rank $r-1$ to $r-\sqrt{r\log r}/8$ in the factor-four coefficient test, for $n\ge d\ge2r$ and $\log r\ge32$. The closest input is the proposed $r-1$ criterion and tilt in PR #185, which this proof uses. The upper-edge row-deficit theorem gives terminal root counts and supplies the near-saturation mass estimate. The absolute eventual theorem supplies no numerical threshold. The rank-dependent theorem uses existential constants and its hypotheses miss the crossover family here. The effective near-linear and near-full sectors also miss that family.
