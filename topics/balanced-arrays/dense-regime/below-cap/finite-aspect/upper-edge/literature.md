# Sources and scope

Per Alexandersson’s unnumbered conjecture asks for real-rootedness for every labeled array size and row cap. In the catalog the section is “Balanced arrays of uniform matroids”. Its small rank-two experiments and matchings connection concern the same generating polynomial. The author's GitHub issue also discusses the first four-column case. The repository subsequently published an all-size proof in that case and an eventual theorem which leaves finitely many possible exceptions across all parameters. Its height threshold is unspecified. These partial theorems leave open which finite parameters, if any, are exceptions. [Original statement](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays) · [Author’s issue](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2005) · [Tracking issue and eligibility record](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/issues/2) · [Eventual theorem](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/pull/101)

Borcea, Brändén and Liggett (2009) supply the stability and negative dependence facts used for a marked row or column in a fixed-margin ensemble. Liebenau and Wormald (2023, online 2022) study nearby degree-sequence counts, including bipartite graphs, by switching. These are precedents for comparing counts. Our earlier regular-margin estimate supplies the quantitative ratio for moving a unit between two prescribed degrees. Here a distinguished row makes that estimate applicable to mixed column quotas. Comparison of the resulting row-deficit distribution with a uniform split bounds the available rows and yields the outer root counts. [Borcea–Brändén–Liggett](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.2340) · [Liebenau–Wormald](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.21105) · [Prior regular-margin result and source comparison](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/blob/14a7c082563830be7b42e5353c2269944a4260b5/topics/balanced-arrays/effective-upper-curvature/regular-margin-ratio-prior.md)
