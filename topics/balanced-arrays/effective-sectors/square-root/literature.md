# Sources and scope

Per Alexandersson's online catalogue states the balanced-array conjecture in the section “Graphs, matroids and arrays.” Its quantifiers allow every height, dimension and admissible row cap. The theorem here proves the full degree of simple negative roots in a restricted region. It supplies a numerical dimension threshold. The stronger existing eventual theorem has one threshold uniform over all dimensions and caps, whose value is unknown. [Original conjecture](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays) · [Eventual theorem](../../eventual-all/README.md) · [Prior eligibility and source checks](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/issues/2)

The two inputs are the orientation proof of log-concavity after division by powers of binomial coefficients, and a lower bound for the spacing of the unrestricted roots on the logarithmic scale. The latter uses Olga Katkova, Boris Shapiro and Anna Vishnyakova, “Multiplier sequences and logarithmic mesh,” online December 22, 2010, in *C. R. Math.* 349 (2011), 35–38, Theorems 4 and 6. The new argument controls the variance at an individual midpoint and joins two root counts. [Orientation proof](../../orientation-normalization.md) · [Unrestricted root spacing](../../dense-regime/below-cap/full-rank-root-stability.md) · [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crma.2010.11.031)
