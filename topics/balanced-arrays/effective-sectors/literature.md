# Sources and scope

Per Alexandersson stated the balanced-array real-rootedness conjecture for uniform matroids. Its original polynomial counts labeled binary arrays with a uniform cap on the row sums and a common total in every column. The present effective bounds concern the same unweighted polynomial. [Original statement](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays) · [Author's tracking thread #2005](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2005).

The validity-probability log-concavity argument reduces to Lemma 8 of Kahn and Neiman's graph-orientation inequality. The finite refinement also uses the negative association of sampling without replacement and preservation under independent concatenation, as in Joag-Dev and Proschan. The theta comparisons use the Jacobi triple product and Gaussian Poisson summation. [Orientation reduction](../orientation-normalization.md) · [Kahn–Neiman](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.0610) · [Joag-Dev–Proschan](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176346079) · [DLMF 20.5.9](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.5.E9) · [DLMF 20.7.33](https://dlmf.nist.gov/20.7.E33).

The earlier [all-cap logarithmic-dimension theorem](../dense-regime/README.md) has unspecified constants. The [uniform eventual theorem](../eventual-all/README.md) has an unspecified row cutoff across all caps and dimensions. The new high-cap result applies at every row count in its sector, and the logarithmic result makes the constants explicit under its cutoff and cap restriction. The [finite result through 35 rows](../finite-residual/README.md) supplies the small-row part of the all-length sector theorem.
