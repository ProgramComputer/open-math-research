# Priority refresh for every fixed positive quadratic aspect

Checked on 2026-11-26, after publication PR #95. The statement under review asks for an eventual threshold $`N_\alpha`$ for **each** $`\alpha>0`$: for all $`n\ge N_\alpha`$, $`d\ge2`$ and $`1\le r\le d`$, the inequality $`n\ge\alpha d^2`$ ensures that the balanced-array polynomial has $`\lfloor nr/d\rfloor`$ simple negative roots. With the earlier rank-dependent dimension theorem, this gives all dimensions at sufficiently large $`n`$ for $`r\le M\log n`$ for **each** fixed $`M>0`$. The original conjecture asks for real-rootedness at every $`n,r,d`$. [Original SymCat conjecture](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays)

## Comparison with prior results

- Publication #95 proves the assertion at one existential absolute aspect constant $`C`$, together with the logarithmic-cap corollary for one existential positive coefficient $`c`$. The present quantifiers cover every positive aspect constant and every fixed positive logarithmic cap coefficient, with thresholds depending on those constants. The earlier proof remains a prerequisite for sequences with $`n/d^2\to\infty`$. [Quadratic theorem and proof at 4fb624b](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/tree/4fb624ba937a8eecc1361a6066185cd81ab7b172/topics/balanced-arrays/quadratic)
- The fixed-cap theorem permits arbitrary dimensions once $`n\ge N_r`$ at each fixed cap. The earlier fixed-pair theorem gives eventual simple zeros and strict consecutive interlacing for proper fixed pairs. Neither statement addresses the new joint dependence on cap and dimension. The present statement is about simplicity and real-rootedness. [Fixed-cap theorem at f7e10f8](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/tree/f7e10f84dd21fec44ca58c60bae0879d12ebd89b/topics/balanced-arrays/fixed-cap) · [Fixed-pair theorem](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/blob/4fb624ba937a8eecc1361a6066185cd81ab7b172/topics/balanced-arrays/fixed-rank-eventual.tex)
- The rank-dependent theorem of #89 gives the complementary dimension range at logarithmic caps. At fixed $`M`$, its lower bound is at most $`B\sqrt{Mn}`$ for large $`n`$, where $`B`$ is the theorem's dimension constant at a fixed admissible exponent. The new aspect threshold covers the other side on taking $`\alpha=1/(B^2M)`$. [Rank-dependent theorem at ba7ddfd](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/tree/ba7ddfd7efb37cac33769f4fdfed749e1b5bf58a/topics/balanced-arrays/dense-regime/rank-dependent)
