# Priority screen: quadratic row bound and logarithmic row caps

Checked 2026-11-26, after [publication PR #93](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/pull/93) merged as 04564a728c21eab6ce511b98a48ccaac754648ff.

For binary $`n\times d`$ labelled arrays with all column sums $`k`$ and row sums at most $`r`$, let $`a_k`$ count them and $`P(q)=\sum_k a_kq^k`$. The candidates are (i) an absolute $`C`$ for which $`n\ge Cd^2`$, uniformly for $`d\ge2`$ and $`1\le r\le d`$, gives exactly $`\lfloor nr/d\rfloor`$ simple negative roots and (ii) absolute $`c>0,N`$ for which the same holds for all $`n\ge N`$, $`d\ge\max(2,r)`$ and $`r\le c\log n`$. The latter would follow by splitting at $`n=Cd^2`$ and applying #89 on the other side. Explicitly, for any fixed $`\varepsilon\in(0,1/5)`$, the rank condition $`c\log n\le n^{1/5-\varepsilon}`$ holds eventually, and $`n<Cd^2`$ implies $`d\ge C_\varepsilon\sqrt{nr/\log n}`$ when $`c\le1/(CC_\varepsilon^2)`$. These are partial results on Alexandersson’s original real-rootedness conjecture for every $`n,r,d`$. [Primary statement](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm#conjBalancedArrays), [author's issue #2005](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2005), [project issue #2](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/issues/2).

The reviewed quartic-log cutoff in #93 is $`C_4d^4\log(d+1)`$ with absolute existential $`C_4`$. Combined with [the rank-dependent #89 theorem](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/pull/89), it does not bridge the dimensions between roughly $`(n/\log n)^{1/4}`$ and $`\sqrt{nr/\log n}`$ uniformly. [Publication #91](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/pull/91) uses an existential threshold for each fixed cap, uniform in dimension, with no growth rate for the threshold as the cap increases. The [fixed-pair theorem](https://github.com/ProgramComputer/open-math-research/blob/ecd64798ac94b6fd81613d1ca6b98ec0d459cb3e/topics/balanced-arrays/fixed-rank-eventual.tex) supplies eventual consecutive interlacing at each fixed proper pair with existential threshold $`N(d,r)`$. The author reports an unreviewed $`U(2,4)`$ eventual argument. The [independent issue #165](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/165) records bounded checks. Fixed-margin enumeration references give asymptotic counts under restricted aspect, degree or density conditions. Those formulas concern prescribed margins, without a theorem about all zeros of the capped polynomial. [GMW](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~csg/papers/irregular-final.pdf), [CGM](https://web.maths.unsw.edu.au/~csg/papers/CGM.pdf), [LW](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/rsa.21105).
