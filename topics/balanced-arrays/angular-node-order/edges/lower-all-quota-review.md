In the manuscript, $t$ is the positive tilt $w$, its constant angle $\phi$ is $\theta=\pi/d$, and $S(t)=H(te^{i\theta})$. Its angular precision is $\lambda(t)=te^{i\theta}B(te^{i\theta})/S(t)$. The sector arguments of $H$ and $B$ are continuous from zero. The expression $\theta+\arg B-\arg H$ is a lift of $\arg\lambda$ and tends to $\theta$ as $t\downarrow0$. For $d\ge3$ this agrees near zero with the manuscript's right-half-plane argument, and continuity and $\Re\lambda>0$ give agreement throughout. Hence its phase $n\arg S-\nu\arg\lambda$ equals $(n+\nu)\arg H-\nu\arg B-\nu\theta$. The polynomial argument is $q=-t^d=-w^d$ in both conventions. With the manuscript's fixed $L,M$, for all sufficiently large $n$ the phase-$L\pi$ and phase-$(D-M)\pi$ tilts lie in the overlap and $D-M>L$. Uniqueness from phase ordering identifies every intervening tilt with $w_k$. At each $L\le k\le D-M$, the overlap estimate gives $(-1)^k C_n P_n(-w_k^d)>1/2$, and $C_n>0$. For $1\le k<L$, the lower transfer has $nw_k\to(\pi k+\nu\theta)/(d\sin\theta)$, the same $U$ used in the strict hyper-Bessel sign theorem. Its signed limit is positive. For $D-M<k<D$, set $j=D-k$, so $1\le j<M$. The upper edge transfer gives the strict sign for each fixed $j$, with a single height threshold across its finitely many attainable residues of $nr$ modulo $d$. Taking the maximum of the finitely many lower and upper thresholds and the uniform overlap threshold proves simultaneity. [Published research edge transfers](phase-edge-transfer.md)

At $d=2$, the row polynomials are $H(z)=1+2z$, $B(z)=1$, and $\nu=1/2$. Thus with $\phi=\arctan(2w)\in(0,\pi/2)$ and $N=n+1/2$ the phase is exactly $N\phi-\pi/4$, strictly increasing to $n\pi/2$. At each internal quota there is one positive tilt. The Legendre representation has a positive multiplier, and $y=\sqrt{\sin\phi}\,L_n(\cos\phi)$ has the same sign as the array polynomial. The Legendre equation gives $y''+(N^2+1/(4\sin^2\phi))y=0$. The vector $(y,-y'/N)$ is nowhere zero on $(0,\pi/2]$ by ODE uniqueness, so its argument admits a continuous lift. For $n=2m$, at $\pi/2$ the vector is horizontal with first-coordinate sign $(-1)^m$. For $n=2m+1$, it is vertical with second-coordinate sign $(-1)^m$, because $y'(\pi/2)=-L'_{2m+1}(0)$. The signs come directly from the constant and linear terms in Rodrigues' formula. These directions permit the lift $\psi(\pi/2)=n\pi/2$, the same value as $N\pi/2-\pi/4$. Its derivative is $N+\cos^2\psi/(4N\sin^2\phi)$, so $|\psi-(N\phi-\pi/4)|\le\cot\phi/(4N)<1/(4N\phi)$. At a node $N\phi=k\pi+\pi/4$, the error is strictly below $\pi/2$, which gives the claimed sign.
